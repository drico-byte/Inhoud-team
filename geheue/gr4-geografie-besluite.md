**Drico, 29 September 2026.** The four video scripts live in
`Marelie videos/Graad 4 SW`. The coverage map against the **official DBE CAPS** is
`kaps/lesindeks/gr4-sosiale-wetenskappe-geografie-dekkingskaart.md` — built before
anything was planned, which is the standing rule.

**The videos already exist.** So Geography runs exactly like Gr 4 History: the video's
lesson division wins, the script is an extra input distilled into a `video_naat`, never
pasted, and the fact checker never sees it. See [[video-se-lesverdeling-wen]].

**30 lessons**, the same as History: Nedersettings 7, Kaartvaardighede 8, Boerdery 6,
Water 9. Year numbers run 1–30 **per discipline**, so Geography starts again at 1.

**400 words a lesson, as close as we can hold it** — the median History budget. The
Grade 4 ceiling of 450 still holds the MEASURED lesson.

**Kaartwerk lesson 1 may be much lower.** It is an introduction that sits in no CAPS
bullet. Drico: *"the text just support the video."* Do not pad it to 400.

**Two CAPS items the scripts miss, and our text carries them — briefly.** Drico:
*"Enough to satisfy caps but not too much."*
* **Jag** — CAPS names five ways people get food (buy, grow, gather, fish, hunt) and the
  script has four.
* **Verbouing van voedsel in dorpe en stede** — CAPS names it beside subsistence and
  commercial farming.
Both are CAPS-named, so they are mandatory and do **not** count against the 25%
supplement cap. A sentence or two each.

**Three chapters need nothing added**: settlements, map skills and water are complete
against CAPS, water impressively so (wetlands, boreholes, water trucks, purification
works, sewage recycling, the return to the sea).

One for the language check: the farming script spells *bestaansboerdery* as
**"bestaanboerdery"** once.
