Grade 5 Lewensvaardighede Skeppende Kunste, built AND SIGNED OFF (8/8) 22 September 2026; drift sweep 40 lessons, zero drift: theory hours only, one lesson per term per strand (Visuele Kunste 1-4, Uitvoerende Kunste 1-4), band 300-550 with Lampies' "hoe korter die les hoe beter solank die kern inhoud oorgedra word". Same method as Grade 4 ([[skeppende-kunste-gr4-teorie-ure]]).

Shared Grade 5 wordings settled at reconciliation and fact-checked read alone: komplementere kleure, klem, fokuspunt, stemming (music, dance, drama and pictures), tempo (speed of the beat), styl, konteks (WITHOUT purpose - CAPS keeps konteks and doel apart).

Grade 5 deliberately departs from delivered Grade 4 wordings:
- tekstuur WIDENED (feel or look) - Grade 5 CAPS adds real objects; Grade 4 stays.
- sekondere kleur: no "in gelyke dele".
- roep-en-antwoord: "een persoon of groep sing of speel".
- konflik (drama): "wat die karakters probeer oplos", not "moet opgelos word".
- verhouding has two meanings in Grade 5 on purpose (OVS relationship vs VK proportion).
