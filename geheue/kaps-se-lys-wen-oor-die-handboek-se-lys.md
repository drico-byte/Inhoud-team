Decided by Drico on 2026-08-21, over two passes — he first ruled one way and reversed on better
information, so the reasoning matters more than the outcome.

**How it came up.** He checked a textbook that says a growing plant needs **four** things — water,
light, warmth, air — while CAPS Gr 4 names **three**: "plante moet lig, water en lug hê om te
groei". Warmth appears in the next CAPS bullet but attached to a **seed**: "saad benodig water en
warmte om te groei". Searched the whole Gr 4-6 document: warmth is never on a growing plant's list
at any of those grades. He asked whether the team had missed a fact.

**First ruling: stay with CAPS's three.** Then he read that a growing plant genuinely does not grow
below a base temperature, and reversed.

**The rule that came out of it, and this is the part to apply:**

> A CAPS-sized list may be incomplete — the lesson never claims completeness, so saying less than
> the whole truth is fine. What it may **not** be is incomplete in a way that creates a **false
> contrast** with another list in the same lesson. If a lesson sets two lists against each other,
> every difference between them must be a real difference.

**Applied here.** The lesson says outright that a seed and a growing plant need different things,
then lists plant = light/water/air and seed = water/warmth. A learner concludes warmth is a
seed-only need (false: plants need warmth) and air is a plant-only need (false: a germinating seed
respires). **No single sentence was untrue — the juxtaposition was.** So warmth was added to the
plant and air to the seed, and the two lists now differ in exactly **one** thing: light. Which is
precisely the distinction CAPS's third bullet exists to make.

**Soil nutrients stay OUT, and that is the rule working rather than an oversight.** Plants do need
them, but nothing in the lesson implies otherwise, so no false contrast exists. Truth alone is not
the trigger; implied denial is.

**What the fact checker did right and should keep doing:** it found the warmth gap itself and
refused to act, on the grounds that changing what the curriculum teaches is a curriculum decision,
not a correction. It flagged and escalated. That is the model for this class of finding — see
[[wanneer-vra-en-wanneer-doen]].

**Recorded as an `aanvulling` with justification** in the approved spec, not smuggled in as a
`kern` item, because CAPS still names three.
