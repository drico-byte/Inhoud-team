29 September 2026, Gr 7 Lewensoriëntering. The same design fault surfaced in three
different sub-topics on one day, found by three different fact checkers. It is a design
fault, not a writing slip, which is why it needs a rule and not three corrections.

**The fault: the no-blame message is hung on a pivot, and the pivot's converse is live.**

| lesson | pivot | what the converse licenses |
|---|---|---|
| Gesondheid 11 | **choice** — "nobody chooses their genes or their street" | blame *is* fair where someone did choose — the lifelong smoker the lesson has just built |
| Self 9 | **a threat** — "where someone threatens you, *then* it is not your fault" | giving in when nobody threatened her *is* her fault |
| Self 12 | **absent** — the block never says whose fault it is | a learner it has already happened to is left with the question open |

The first one was my own repair. I had taken a *quantifier* out of that block a day earlier
and put the rule on choice instead, and felt I had fixed it.

**The test, and it costs nothing:** read the no-blame sentence alone and ask what its
converse permits. If it carries an *omdat*, a *waar* or a *dan*, the converse is live and a
learner can derive it. A sentence with no pivot has no converse.

**The form:** the message is *stated*, not motivated. Where a hard case does need answering,
pick a reason that is not a condition:

- a **consequence** works — "blame measurably makes it harder to go for help and to stay in
  treatment" — because it has no converse;
- a **condition of the person** works — "dependence is itself something that needs treatment";
- a reason resting on **choice, capacity, a threat or circumstances** does not.

**Two corollaries that cost rounds here:**

- Where a lesson asks a child to *do* something — say no, get away, tell — it must also say
  she is not at fault if she could not. Without that sentence the step becomes a standard she
  measures herself against. This is why prevention research rejects teaching a child to refuse
  sexual abuse: the advice puts the responsibility on the child.
- These sentences usually work as a **set**, not alone — eight such sets were found in two
  rights lessons. A later revision that cuts one half makes the block false. Say in the field
  that a set exists.
