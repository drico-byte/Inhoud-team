Lampies, 14 October 2026: "the only thing that ever reaches the learners is the actual content of each lesson."

The PDF prints a box at the top quoting CAPS's own topic line word for word (labelled "the curriculum's words, not the lesson's claim"). For Gr 6 Mapungubwe that line says "eerste staat in Suider-Afrika 1220 - 1300". That box, the status stamp and every other metadata field are team-facing only.

**Why:** fact checkers flag the CAPS line as an uncertain superlative on every Mapungubwe lesson. Whether it misleads a learner was the open question; it cannot, because no learner sees it.

**How to apply:** when a fact report holds a lesson up only on the CAPS-point quotation, the risk is nil for learners. It still needs a per-lesson acceptance record (no blanket form exists). Write in that record that the line is printed in the team PDF under the curriculum-quote label and never reaches learners. Do not write "it is not in the PDF"; lesson 4's record said that, and it is false.

Keep explanations to Lampies at plain level: one question, yes/no where possible. They found the acceptance-record mechanics confusing late at night. See [[rapporteer-in-gewone-taal]].
