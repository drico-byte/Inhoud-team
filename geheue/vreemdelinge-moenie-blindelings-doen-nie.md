Lampies, 18 Sep 2026, on Gr 5 Life Skills "Die mense in jou lewe": "the point is to teach them that they should not blindly follow and do what strangers tell them."

The planner had written the strangers part defensively, leading with "we cannot know what a stranger wants" and warning against teaching that danger comes from strangers. The warning is true — a child's risk usually sits with people the family knows — but it had swallowed the lesson's actual purpose.

**Why:** a caution against a false claim is not a reason to drop a true behaviour rule. The stranger rule is about what a child DOES; the risk fact is about where danger comes FROM. Both hold.
**How to apply:** lead with the rule (don't go with someone, don't get in a car, don't take a gift, don't believe a message from your parents, even if the stranger is friendly or asks for help; a child may say no and walk away), then say the same markers apply to people the child knows.
