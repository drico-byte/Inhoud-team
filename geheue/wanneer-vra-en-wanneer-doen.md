Straightforward factual corrections with an unambiguous source: apply them, do not
ask. Confirmed 2026-08-19, when four contradicted claims in lesson 1 were corrected
without asking alongside the three decisions Drico had made — he called that the
right call and asked me to keep exercising that judgement rather than checking on
every obvious fix.

Bring to him: anything involving a trade-off, a curriculum decision, or a change to
the content standard.

**Why:** asking about every obvious fix wastes his attention and slows the pipeline,
while silently making a curriculum or standard decision takes control of the design
away from the person who owns it.

**How to apply:** the test is whether judgement is being exercised, not whether the
change is large. A wrong date with a clear source is a fix. Cutting a sentence to
fit a budget, rewording what a lesson must deliver, or altering a rule in the
standard is a decision. Related: [[rapporteer-in-gewone-taal]]
