Seen twice in one sitting on Gr 4 NWT Term 4, 2026-08-28, and it will recur.

**The shape.** The fact checker finds that a block lets a learner reason on to something
false, and requires a qualifier to close it. The writer adds it. The coverage checker
then lists that same qualifier under `oortollig`, because no `kern` item, no
`aanvulling` and no flagged concept asks for it — which is true, and which is exactly
what that checker is for.

**Two live examples.**

- *Die Son.* The car-lights analogy let a child conclude that dim stars are the far
  ones. The fix was two sentences saying stars differ in how much light they give off.
  Coverage flagged both as unrequested.
- *Die Maan, les 1.* The apparent-size claim pinned the match on distance alone, which
  is false — something 400 times farther looks smaller. The fix ties the width ratio and
  the distance ratio together. Coverage flagged the width ratio and the general rule
  about distance as surplus.

**The ruling: keep the caveat, and do not send it back.** Cutting reinstates an error a
sourced check already caught. Coverage is right that nothing asked for it and right to
record it; its own prompt says `oortollig` is an observation and never changes a verdict.
The two checkers are answering different questions and neither is wrong.

**Why it happens, and why it is not a bug.** The spec is written before the draft, so it
cannot know which sentence will invite a false inference. Only a sourced check on real
prose finds that. So a correct lesson ends up slightly over-supplied against its spec,
and that is the cheaper failure. Compare [[verklaar-moenie-net-dek-nie]]: covering a
requirement and teaching something false is the expensive one.

**What to do with it.** Note it and move on. Where the caveat is large enough to matter
against the budget, the fix belongs in the spec at the next revision, not in the draft —
see [[moenie-n-regstelling-verder-vat-as-die-bevinding-nie]] for why the draft is the
wrong place to settle it. Related: [[n-regstelling-ontwrig-sy-bure]].
