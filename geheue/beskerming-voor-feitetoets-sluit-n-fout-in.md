On 28 October 2026 I twice protected a wording that turned out to be false, both times because a coordinator had passed on the writer's predicted swap before the fact check had read it:

- Gr 6 NW Bewegings: "draai elkeen sy kante om die beurt na die Son". This is false for Uranus.
- Gr 6 UK 2: "maak elke beweging op dieselfde oomblik". Pantsula is also improvised.

Each time, the protection blocked the very fix the fact check asked for, and I had to withdraw it first.

**Why:** a protected wording reads as settled to everyone downstream, but a writer's brand-new wording has been checked by nobody yet.

**How to apply:**
- Before protecting a phrase, ask whether it carries a fact claim that has not yet passed a fact check.
  - If it does, protect only the part that is a word choice (the term or the register), or say in the rede that the protection lapses if the fact check rejects it.
  - Protect quantifiers and corrections only once a fact check has confirmed them.
- When a fact check rejects a protected phrase, withdraw or replace the protection in the same breath as the spec fix.

**Third time, same day:** Gr 6 SW Mapungubwe 3. I protected whole sentences to keep "verbou" in place of "kos maak". The sentences also carried a new "elke gesin", and the fact check could not support it. **A word choice inside a sentence that makes a new claim gets a word-level entry (hou = the word), never the whole sentence.**
