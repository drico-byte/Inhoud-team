**Drico, 7 October 2026, for all future subjects:** "When a map is needed for support, the content
must make a note of it. The html team will then handle the visuals to ensure that it give the
needed visual support for the content written." He said he had said it before.

**It was recorded nowhere.** On 7 Oct 2026 nothing in the standard, the schema, the planner or the
writer carried it, and the writer prompt (skrywer-v1.6) said the opposite: "Do not describe
visuals or suggest how to render anything." That is why it got lost — a ruling given in chat and
not written into the file the writer reads is undone by the file.

**How to apply:** in a layout, mark every lesson whose text leans on geography (routes, places,
frontiers, distributions). The note says WHAT the map must show (places, routes, the relationship
the text explains), never how to draw it — rendering stays the HTML team's call.
