**5 October 2026.** A coverage report on lesson 8 came back with two things: a spec
fault and one unrequested sentence. I repaired the spec and sent a writer round for the
sentence — while a fact check on that same lesson was still running. The writer changed
the draft, so the fact check was reading a copy that no longer matched, and a
forty-minute check landed on text that had already moved.

**Why it is easy to do:** a coverage finding and a fact finding arrive separately, and
each one on its own looks like something to act on immediately. The pipeline's own
freshness test is what punishes it — a report older than the draft is stale, however
sound its findings — so the check is not merely redundant, it is unusable for sign-off.

**How to apply:** per lesson, only one of writer / coverage / facts is in flight at a
time. When a coverage finding arrives while facts is still running, hold the writer round
until facts lands and brief the writer on BOTH, which is cheaper anyway: one round, one
gate, one pair of re-checks. Across different lessons, parallel is fine and is how the
work gets done. Related: [[moenie-die-hardloper-oor-n-werkende-skrywer-laat-loop-nie]]
(the same collision from the runner's side), [[die-feitenasiener-lees-n-verouderde-konsep]].

The exception worth taking: a SAFETY correction does not wait for a check to finish.
Burning a check is cheaper than leaving the sentence standing.
