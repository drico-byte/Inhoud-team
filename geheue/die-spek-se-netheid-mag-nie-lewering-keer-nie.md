13 October 2026. Gr 6 SW Medicine lesson 5 was finished by late morning: every requirement
present, nothing surplus, fact check clean at 32 of 32. **I did not sign it off for four
more coverage rounds**, because each round returned MENS_NODIG over faults in the
*specification prose* — a count read off the draft, a sentence describing another file's
state, a record speaking in the present tense. Not one of them was a defect in the lesson.
Nothing a child would read changed in any of those rounds.

Lampies stopped me: *"im really struggling to understand how this is so difficult to
finalize. We are not writing a thesis... maybe our goals has become a bit foggy."*

**The goal.** CAPS-aligned Afrikaans lessons a child can read, carried through checks and
sign-off to delivery. A specification is a tool for producing them. It is not a
deliverable, and its tidiness is not a quality gate on a finished lesson.

**The two mistakes, separately, because they compound.**

1. **I treated a specification finding as a blocker.** Those findings describe risk to a
   FUTURE revision — a stale description could mislead the next writer. That is worth
   fixing. It is not worth withholding a lesson that is already correct. Ask of every
   finding: *could this make someone change THIS lesson wrongly?* If no, it is a logged
   tidy-up, not a gate.
2. **I fixed two or three findings per round and re-checked.** Each round then found two or
   three more, several of them introduced by the previous round's own prose. Four rounds.
   When I finally fixed all ten in one pass — with the descriptive-claim guard run over
   every field afterwards — the next check came back GOEDGEKEUR first time.

**How to apply.**

* **Separate the two verdicts out loud.** When a coverage report is MENS_NODIG, say
  immediately whether any item is `afwesig` or `gedeeltelik`. If none is, the lesson is
  complete and the escalation is about the spec — say so in that sentence, not three
  paragraphs later.
* **Batch spec repair.** Never fix a subset and re-check. Collect every finding, fix them
  in one pass, run the guard over the whole entry, then check once.
* **Brief the checker on proportion.** Telling a coverage checker to set the
  specification flag only where something could actually change this lesson wrongly — and
  to report mere untidiness in the summary instead — is what turned the fifth round into a
  pass. It is not lowering the bar; it is putting the finding where it belongs.
* **And watch for this shape in myself:** the work felt productive the whole time, because
  every round found something real. Finding something real is not the same as the work
  being worth doing now. Related: [[loop-die-hele-proses-tot-by-goedkeuring]],
