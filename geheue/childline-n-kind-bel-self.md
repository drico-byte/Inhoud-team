Checked 18 Sep 2026 against Childline SA and government sources: the 116 line is toll-free from every network, runs 24 hours, and a child speaks to a counsellor confidentially. No adult is needed.

Two Gr 5 Life Skills lessons had written it as something a teacher or nurse helps you phone.

**Why:** the child who most needs it is the one whose adults at home are the problem. An adult-assisted route closes the only door that was open to her.
**How to apply:** name the child's own route wherever Childline appears; an adult helping may stand beside it, never instead of it. Still no phone number in any lesson — numbers go stale ([[n-les-met-n-nommer-in-verouder]]). If one is ever printed, note that the toll-free number is 116 while Childline's own homepage still shows the older 0800 55 555, so it must be confirmed first.
