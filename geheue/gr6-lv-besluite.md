Lampies started Grade 6 Lewensvaardighede (PSW) on 21 September 2026, from the workbook "Intersen LO", sheet G6-PSW, and CAPS pp. 25-28 (PDF pages 30-33).

1. **Length: 550 words per lesson, band 450-550**, one step up from Grade 5. Now in the content standard and in spec_check.
2. **Reading pieces are stories**, as in Grade 5. CAPS's Grade 6 wording ("interpreteer/verduidelik en vertel wat aangeleer is") changed, but the ruling did not.
3. **The risky specs are fact-checked BEFORE any writing starts**: first aid, gender stereotyping/sexism/abuse, HIV, bullying, food hygiene, diseases. This is a process change for this grade, because in Grade 5 most errors began in the spec.
4. **16 CAPS blocks give exactly 32 lessons**, with no extra lessons. Split: Ontwikkeling van self 12, Sosiale verantwoordelikheid 12, Gesondheid en omgewing 8.
5. **The workbook sheet has two slips, and CAPS wins**: it cites pp. 21-24 (that is Grade 5), and it puts lessons 13-16 under Ontwikkeling van self where CAPS puts them under Sosiale verantwoordelikheid.
6. **Exceptions are proposed AFTER planning**, as in Grade 5. The expected one is first aid: 1.5 CAPS hours for eight situations.
7. **Religions**: the same seven as Grades 4 and 5 by default, unless Lampies decides otherwise.

8. **First aid got the exception: 625 words** (Lampies, 21 Sep 2026), the only one in Grade 6. Cutting to 550 would have cost safety lines.
9. **Signs and symptoms, Grades 5-6** (Lampies, 21 Sep 2026): "simptoom" is only what you feel because you are ill; "teken" is what the illness changes that others can see or measure. Items the sources dispute (vomiting, diarrhoea, a cough, a dry mouth) are never labelled one way - they are named together as "tekens en simptome". A TB cough is "die belangrikste waarskuwing". Both grades' agreed-wordings files carry this.
10. **Other settled wordings** (all in the protected-words file): noodhulp, not "eerstehulp"; call an adult AT THE SAME TIME as cooling or pressing, never "first"; press before rinsing a cut; cling film, not cloth, over a burn; gloves for blood or other body fluids, not just "a wound"; care is "the work of adults", not what every home does; "dreig om seer te maak", never plain "dreig"; food labels in English ("Use by", "Best before").
11. **Lesson-local safety sentences that keep generating findings get cut, not patched** - the "ordinary safety rules are not abuse" add-on in SV 11 went after three rounds; the definition alone already excluded an ordinary parent.

**How to apply:** the year plan and every note in it are in the Grade 6 plan file under `kaps/`. The Grade 6 agreed-wordings file carries over the Grade 5 terms, and a new lesson copies them word for word. See [[gr5-lv-besluite]].
