Lampies, 22 September 2026: Creative Arts lessons cover only the content (the theory hours: Visual Literacy and Appreciation and Reflection). The practical work (2D/3D making, warm-ups, improvising, performing) is not included in the lessons for now. This applies to all grades, 4 to 6.

**Why:** the practical work belongs to the class; the text teaches the thinking the practical tests (see [[praktiese-werk-ons-leer-die-denke]]).

**How to apply:** plan one lesson per term per art form from the theory topics only; a lesson may mention this term's practical work as the thing learners talk about, but never teaches how to make or perform it. See [[skeppende-kunste-gr4-teorie-ure]], [[gr5-skeppende-kunste]].
