On 22 October 2026 a fact check of Gr 4 NW year lesson 13 said sawing a log into planks counts as processing, so the shared 'verwerkte materiaal' wording ("... sodat dit ander eienskappe kry") was too narrow. I changed the shared wording and sent lesson 12 (signed) to the writer. Then I found the 28 August ruling in the spec: an earlier fact check had settled the opposite (sawing changes shape, not material). I reverted both and put the conflict to Lampies.

**Why:** two checkers reading the sources differently is exactly the "sources genuinely conflict" case ([[bronne-weerspreek-mekaar-dek-ons-met-een-sin]]); acting on the newer one silently overturns a ruling and starts a flip-flop across lessons.

**How to apply:** before changing a shared wording or protected wording on a fact finding, grep the spec and `kaps/beskermde-woorde.json` for a dated ruling on the same point. If one exists and disagrees, it goes to Lampies (accept-by-name or a ruling), not to the writer. Related: [[die-ooreengekome-bewoording-kan-self-verkeerd-wees]], [[wanneer-vra-en-wanneer-doen]].
