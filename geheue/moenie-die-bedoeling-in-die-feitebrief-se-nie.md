18 September 2026. The eli10 pre-check brief for frame structures said the word
"buite om" was *deliberate*, since a ribcage has muscle over it. The checker
called this out: the brief had given it the writer's intent, which is exactly what
the fact copy has its provenance note removed to withhold. It judged the text anyway
and found that "buite om" doesn't do what I claimed. The sentence is literally
true of ribs too. The real distinguishing point is that a crab's shell *is* the
outside of its body, with nothing over it.

**Why it matters:** the runner strips the draft, and then I put the intent
straight back in through the brief. A less careful checker would have read the block
charitably, which is how three rounds of this lesson went before.

**How to apply:** in a fact or eli10 brief, say what to test and which cases to try
("does it hold for a ribcage, a skull, a tortoise?"). Never say why a word was chosen or
what it is supposed to exclude. That belongs in the writer's brief and the spec.
