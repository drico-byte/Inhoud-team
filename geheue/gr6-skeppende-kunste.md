Grade 6 Lewensvaardighede Skeppende Kunste, built and signed off (8/8) on 22 September 2026. Theory hours only, one lesson per term per strand ([[skeppende-kunste-net-inhoud]]). The band is 300-550 like Grade 5 Creative Arts, not Grade 6's 450-550; lessons under 450 carry a vloer_uitsondering citing Lampies. The drift sweep read 40 lessons and found zero drift.

**Lampies' choices from the CAPS lists:**
- drama (term 1): lofdig and kinderteater
- dance (term 2): pantsula and Indiese dans, because Grade 5 already did gumboot, ballet and kwaito
- music (term 3): isicathamiya and maskandi
- the balance term is **straalvormige balans**, not "radiale balans"

**Wordings Grade 6 widened or set:**
- konteks now covers music, and where something is performed today
- patroon is the regular ORDER of the repeats, not the thing repeated. Its examples after "soos" may differ per lesson (voorbeelde_mag_verskil).
- balans: symmetric, asymmetric and straalvormig. A part only *looks* heavy.
- ruimte is NOT new in Grade 6. CAPS uses it from Grade 4 in clay work; what is new is spotting it in a flat picture.

**Why:** fact checks caught overclaims repeatedly: unity as "the" new principle, space "gets a name now", maskandi's concertina as a later addition, soft-versus-loud in isicathamiya, and the class drama written in the past tense when the lesson falls mid-term.

**How to apply:** reuse these wordings in later arts work.

**Open question for Lampies** (not yet asked): Grade 4's agreed "vorm" (a flat area a line encloses) is too narrow, and "patroon" wrongly calls the shape the pattern. Both sit in delivered Grade 4 and 5 lessons. Grade 6 VK 3 carries no vorm entry for now. See [[gr5-skeppende-kunste]].
