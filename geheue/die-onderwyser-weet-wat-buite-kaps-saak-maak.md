Drico, 7 September 2026, after I put a choice to him about Gr 4 SW Kwartaal 1: **"A is
fine, carry on. Thats the power of having a teacher in the mix... They know whats
important beyond the caps points."**

## The choice he was ruling on

CAPS lists Kwartaal 1's content as essentially one item — four ways to find out about
the past. The teacher's five videos cover that in two of them; the other three teach
what history is, the vocabulary of time (v.C./n.C., centuries, timelines), and why
history is worth learning.

Read against the CAPS **content table** alone, those three are `aanvulling` at 60% of
the sub-topic against a 25% cap, and the specification fails. Read against the **whole**
CAPS document they are delivery, because CAPS lists its historical aims, skills and
concepts in a separate section and says following them is *"van kritieke belang ... vir
elke inhoudsonderwerp"*.

I proposed the second reading and he took it.

## The principle

**When our rules and the teacher's judgement disagree, look for the part of CAPS our
rule never read before concluding she is wrong.** The 25% cap was written when coverage
meant the content table. It had never met a subject where the curriculum splits content
from skills across two sections.

This is the same shape as [[video-se-lesverdeling-wen]] — her lesson seams carried
teaching information a bullet list does not — and it is the second time her judgement
has beaten a rule of ours.

**Where it stops.** A lesson serving neither the content table nor the skills section is
still supplement, still needs a justification, and still counts against the cap. The
ruling widened what counts as CAPS delivery; it did not remove the cap.

## And the wider ruling from the same conversation

**"This whole SW inhoud we are creating is going to be a different process, and we must
treat it as an exception to what we have been doing the whole time."** The six departures
are gathered under "Gr 4 Sosiale Wetenskappe runs a different process" in
`skills/wolkskool-inhoudstandaard/SKILL.md` rather than left as caveats sprinkled through
rules the subject does not follow. Add to that list rather than patching another rule.
