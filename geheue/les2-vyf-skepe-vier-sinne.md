Decided with Drico on 2026-08-19, before lesson 2 was written.

CAPS names five vessel types in that bullet, so all five are mandatory, plus how a
sail uses wind. Measured from lesson 1, a "what is X" vessel block costs 55 words at
five sentences. Five ships at that depth plus a mechanism block projects to ~330 words
against a 257 budget with a 296 hard ceiling — about 13% over. At three to four
sentences each it lands near 275 and passes.

**Decision: keep it as one lesson, four sentences per ship.** Drico: "Thats a small
trade. 4 sentences are plenty."

**Why not split:** splitting divides the sub-topic's measured volume by a larger
lesson count, so a fifth lesson would drop all four others from 257 to about 206 —
and lesson 1 already measures 291. It would break a finished lesson to make room in an
unwritten one.

**How to apply:** the writer's instructions now carry the general rule — when a bullet
names several items, divide the budget by the item count before writing, and treat the
standard's three-sentence pattern as the ceiling rather than the floor. Also consider
folding the sail mechanism into the ship blocks rather than giving it its own, which
buys back roughly 50 words if needed.
