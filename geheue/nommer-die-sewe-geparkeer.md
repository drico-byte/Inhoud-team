**Outcome, 2026-08-20: the test passed and the question is closed.** Drico's words: "The html
designer knew that it had to be numbered. Im actually impressed."

**What was tested.** Gr 4 NWT lesson 1 has ten study headings. Seven are the life processes; the
other three are the opening, things that don't look alive, and germs. Every heading looked
structurally identical, and the lesson repeatedly says *al sewe* — so a learner was told to count
something the page gave them no way to count. Drico's fear was that the HTML team would not render
those seven as a set.

**The test he chose:** hand lesson 1 over with no guidance at all and see whether they grouped the
seven anyway. Prompting them would have voided it. They did group it, unprompted.

**What that means.** The signal is already implicit in the text — sequential headings each naming
one process, and a running count the lesson states out loud. A human reading it can see the set.
**So no change to the lesson format is needed:** no numbered-set field, no numbers typed into
headings, no instruction to the writer. Do not reopen this.

**The design reasoning, kept only in case a future case is genuinely different:** the argument for
marking a set structurally was that a heading is already an identifier (the coverage report quotes
it; an intuition block points at its study block by exact heading match), so a number inside a
heading becomes part of that identity; and a typed number welds presentation into content, so a
later rendering change would mean editing every lesson. The argument against error-risk was weak
and was withdrawn — a check on typed numbers is crude but possible.

**Second instance, 2026-08-21.** Gr 4 NWT lesson 1's only evidence that yeast is alive (bubbles in
warm sugar water) sat inside a question, and questions are no longer written. I flagged it as a gap
and proposed moving the sentence. Drico: "The html team made it practical, showing how it comes
alive anyways. Dont stress. That team is on fire." They had built the observable as an interaction.

**Do NOT weaken the rule for new lessons on the strength of this.** Evidence for a claim still
belongs in the study text, because the text is the floor the rendered lesson honours and it has to
stand alone as the revision instrument -- see [[teks-loop-parallel-met-n-video]]. The layout team
turning a claim into something interactive is a bonus on top of a complete text, not a substitute
for one. What this instance changes is only whether an OLD lesson needs retro-fixing: it does not.

**The generalisable lesson, which is the part worth keeping:** before building structure into the
lesson format to make something explicit, check whether the downstream team already infers it.
They inferred it here. Related: [[teks-loop-parallel-met-n-video]]
