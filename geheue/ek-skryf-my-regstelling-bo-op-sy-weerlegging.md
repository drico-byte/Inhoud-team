9 September 2026, Gr 4 Sosiale Wetenskappe, the local-history lesson on source
types. I ruled that only three of the four kinds of source answer the
present-day question, and that archaeological sources answer only the past. I
propagated it into three fields, including one whose **own first sentence says
the opposite**: the curriculum's first two headings each print the same four
bullets, "information from objects" among them. CAPS puts objects under the
present-day heading. My correction sat directly beneath the sentence that
refutes it, in the same field, and I never read that sentence.

A fact check found it from the other end, in the draft: photos, writings and
stories for now, all four for long ago — every sentence true, the contrast
false. Objects are evidence about the present; that is the whole basis of
material culture study and of contemporary archaeology. A child asked how to
find out about their own area today would answer "not from objects".

**What made this different from an ordinary wrong ruling.** The refutation was
not in a source I failed to search. It was in the file, in the field, one line
up. I was in append mode: I had a correction to record, I found the field that
carried the claim, and I wrote at the bottom of it. `n-feiterisiko-is-nie-n-
regstelling-nie` had already taught me to amend the **opening line** rather than
append — and I had spent that same hour applying that rule to four other
fields. Amending the opening line would have forced me to read it.

**How to apply.** Before writing a correction into a field, read the whole field
first and ask what it already says about the thing you are about to assert. If
the fix is worth writing, it is worth thirty seconds of reading the paragraph it
lands in. And when a ruling is mine rather than a checker's, it has had no
check at all — see [[spesifikasies-word-nooit-nagegaan]] and
[[moenie-in-die-spek-skryf-wat-jy-nie-nagegaan-het-nie]].

**The cost.** One wrong ruling, three fields, one wasted writer brief, and a
reversal that had to be recorded in all three places so a later revision does
not reinstate it. Cheap this time only because a fact check ran on the lesson
the same day.
