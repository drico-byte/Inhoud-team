**Drico, 7 October 2026:** "I dont mind if our inhoud has more words than a textbook would, it just
mean that we give more educational content. But im scared for the places where we give too little."
Said after a long day of rate calls (Gr 6 NWT), which he found hard to make.

**How to apply:**
- When a budget call is uncertain, take the generous side. Do not trim a topic to match a book.
- Shortfall is the risk to hunt for. A word count against hours cannot find it on its own — the
  Gr 6 NWT "short" lessons were complete and near a textbook's count. What finds it is depth per
  CAPS content point and assessed item: explained, or only named?
- Do not hand him a stream of hard calls; turn them into defaults with one safety valve each.
