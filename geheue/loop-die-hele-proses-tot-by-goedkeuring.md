**Drico, 2 September 2026:** "from now on you have to complete the whole
process (up to the sign off). Export as pdfs then."

He had assumed 29 drafted-and-gated lessons meant the subject was finished. It
did not — the coverage and fact checks had not run, nothing was approved, and
the lessons could not go to his language editor.

**Why:** "drafted" is not a deliverable to him. A lesson he can act on is one
that has been checked for coverage, checked for facts, repaired, signed off, and
exported as a PDF he can send out. Stopping at drafted looks like completion and
is not, so he has to ask for the rest.

**How to apply:** for any subject or term, carry it through the whole pipeline
without being asked again — draft, gate, reconcile shared terms, coverage and
fact checks, repair the findings at the spec as well as the lesson, sign off
(see [[outo-goedkeuring-is-die-laaste-stap]]), then export PDFs with
`bin/leeskopie.py --alles`. Escalations still stop, and a genuine trade-off is
still his call — but the default is to finish.

The language check happens **after** all of that and outside this pipeline, so
never hand him PDFs before the fact checks have run: he pays his editor twice
and the second pass edits text the fact check already changed.
