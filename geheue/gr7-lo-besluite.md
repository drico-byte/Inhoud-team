Grade 7 Lewensoriëntering, **without Liggaamsopvoeding**. Four sub-topics, all four specs approved by Lampies on 23 September 2026: Selfontwikkeling in die samelewing (14 lessons, 7 700 words), Gesondheids-, sosiale en omgewingsverantwoordelikheid (13, 7 130), Grondwetlike regte en verantwoordelikhede (9, 5 450), Wêreld van werk (9, 5 030).

**Lampies' rules for Grades 7-9:** lessons are the learner's own working text; one grade at a time; the lesson count comes from the content; 350-700 words, set by the content, nothing superfluous. The band is now in the standard for Grades 7, 8 and 9.

**His rulings this round:** puberty named plainly (menstruasie, ereksies, natmaakdrome) and stopping there; groepsdruk widened for Grade 7; alcohol, tobacco and e-cigarettes named, no illegal drug; the epileptic-seizure steps are **eight** (adding head cushion, stay until fully awake, nothing to eat or drink) with the side position AFTER the seizure; Childline named without a number; "reeds in jou" stays in potensiaal; Grade 6 science keeps CAPS's four food groups; the protein-cost point stays out; the bullying boundary is stated once, in lesson 9.

**What the pre-writing fact checks cost and bought.** Every spec was checked before a lesson was written: 58 findings across the four, including in my own corrections. Worth knowing:
- section 36 is "algemeen geldende **regsvoorskrif**", "redelik en regverdigbaar" — and "billik" simply does not appear there (do not claim it belongs to the equality clause)
- "Handves van Regte", not "van Menseregte", even where CAPS writes the latter
- a **police official is not** an s110(1) mandated reporter; the duty binds teacher, nurse, social worker, minister of religion, and anyone for a sexual offence under s54
- the current term is "temporary safe care", not "plek van veiligheid"
- the Constitution came into force 4 February 1997; *S v Makwanyane* was under the interim Constitution, so the court example stays unnamed
- **no age law covers e-cigarettes** in South Africa; the rule is still a bill
- only TB has a course you finish — never say "maak 'n kuur klaar" of HIV, epilepsy or diabetes
- erections are **not** a change of puberty; ejaculation and increased frequency are
- a nutrition table is not required on SA prepackaged food unless a claim is made
- the SA dietary guidelines are an **unnumbered** list of eleven and three official documents order them differently — quote the words, never a number
