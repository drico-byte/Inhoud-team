Gr 5 Lewensvaardighede, 18-19 Sep 2026: across 32 lessons and 64 checks, seventeen faults turned out to live in the specification, not in the draft. Examples: the spec ordered "an adult helps you phone Childline" in four places; it defined a violent situation as people hurting "each other"; it said paraffin's contents do not look like a drink; it demanded "no number of days" for when TB stops being infectious, which made the sentence false; it required every named substance to be called illegal when glue is a shop product; it banned the rehydration recipe with a prohibition that was itself untrue.

**Why:** a draft fix alone does not hold — coverage tests against the spec, so a corrected draft then FAILS, and the next revision writes the fault back in. Writers flagged nearly all of these themselves, in the form "I fixed the lesson; the spec still orders the error."

**How to apply:** when a checker finding names a claim, grep the spec for it before briefing the writer, and sweep every field that repeats it ([[n-spek-se-dieselfde-ding-in-twee-velde]]). Amend the field's OPENING LINE, never append a note ([[n-feiterisiko-is-nie-n-regstelling-nie]]). Assert every swap and re-read the field as prose afterwards ([[n-laslap-binne-n-sin-breek-die-veld-as-prosa]]). And take a writer's "this needs fixing at source" seriously every time — it was right every time.
