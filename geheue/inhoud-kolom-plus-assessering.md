**Drico, 7 October 2026:** "we create content only on the inhoud en konsepte column in the caps
document, right? And then make sure that everything listed in what will be assessed is covered."

**Not new — Drico: it has been the process since the team began.** The assessment half is his
standing instruction of 21 August 2026 ([[kruistoets-die-termynplan-teen-kaps]]). On 7 October I
checked: the planner prompt is handed "content bullets" (the content-column half, implicitly), but
NEITHER half is written into the planner, the coverage checker or the standard. It lives in practice
and in these notes only — the same "exists but you must know to look" weakness.

**The rule, in two halves:**
1. **Source:** only the *Inhoud en Konsepte* column. The *Voorgestelde Aktiwiteite* column is not
   content, even where it names a concept (pickling, SALT, rover parts, ecosystem threats).
2. **Check:** every item in the term's *Toets leerder se kennis deur te sien of hulle* list must be
   covered by some lesson. Where an assessed item exists only in the activities column (Gr 6 NWT:
   the TV-to-power-station-to-Sun path; the Sun/Earth/Moon comparison; SALT and SKA), the assessment
   list pulls it in. Hands-on assessed items (design, make, grow) get the thinking only — see
   [[praktiese-werk-ons-leer-die-denke]].

**Why it matters:** in a Gr 6 NWT layout I mixed both columns and nearly called two old lessons
deficient for "missing" items that exist only in the activities column. Under this rule they were
complete, just short.

**Known exception:** Gr 4 Sosiale Wetenskappe also reads CAPS's skills-and-concepts section
(CLAUDE.md). Related: [[kruistoets-die-termynplan-teen-kaps]], [[n-plat-plafon-verdeel-volgens-les-nie-onderwerp]].
