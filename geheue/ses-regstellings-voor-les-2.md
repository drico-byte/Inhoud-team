All six fixes agreed with Drico were completed on 2026-08-19, before lesson 1 was
approved rather than after, because lesson 1 had already spent both its revision
cycles and so could not go back to the writer regardless.

The writer's instructions now say the intuition layer is free in words but is the
highest factual-risk block in the lesson, and that limitations are comparative
rather than absolute. The planner's instructions now ask for a lesson's
*contribution* to the focus question, sized to its budget. The coverage checker now
records evidence as a block name plus one clause rather than a paragraph. The report
validator no longer false-alarms about glossary entries. And the standard's script
paths now resolve from where agents actually run.

**The fact that does not live in the repository:** lesson 1 was written under the
OLD writer instructions and then hand-corrected for exactly the failures the new
ones now prevent — six errors in the intuition block, two false absolutes in the
bullet list. So lesson 1 is not a fair sample of what the current prompts produce.
When judging whether the fixes worked, compare lessons 2 to 4 against each other,
not against lesson 1.

**How to apply:** if the intuition block or a bullet-list absolute goes wrong again
in lessons 2 to 4, the reworded instruction did not take and the next step is a
harder constraint, not a third rewording.
