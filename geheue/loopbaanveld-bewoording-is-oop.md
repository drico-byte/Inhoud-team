29 September 2026, Grade 7 LO, Wêreld van werk. The shared `loopbaanveld` wording is
**open and must not be changed again until a person decides.** It currently reads that
careers are grouped because the same kind of knowledge and abilities are asked, *while
the tasks and the interests differ from one career to the next*.

**I wrote that this morning, on one fact check, and the afternoon's fact check
contradicts it.**

- **Morning (Werk 4):** interests differ sharply *inside* one field — a nurse's profile
  is helping, teaching, talking; a medical records clerk's is data, detail, routine;
  dental laboratory work is handwork. So interests were moved off the shared side.
- **Afternoon (Werk 3):** CAPS itself attaches interests **to** the field. Grade 7:
  "Career fields: Qualities relating to each field: interests and abilities." Grade 8:
  "Interests and abilities related to each career category." And the **six** fields this
  sub-topic uses are Holland's groups, which are built ON interest — so the wording says
  interests differ between careers that were grouped by interest. Three later blocks in
  Werk 3 already contradict it by letting an interest point at a field.
- The **first half is unsettled, not wrong**: grouping on knowledge and skill is real
  (ISCO-08 groups on skill level and specialisation) but yields eight to ten major
  groups, never six. The sentence gives one taxonomy's criterion for another taxonomy's
  groups.

**Where the answer probably lies:** a FIELD is partly defined by interest, while the
TASKS differ from career to career. The morning check is right about individual careers;
the afternoon check is right about what the field *is*. That puts interests back on the
shared side and leaves only "the tasks" on the differing side. But **which grouping the
sub-topic teaches is a curriculum decision**, so it is Lampies' call, not mine.

## Ruled, the same day

**Lampies: "belangstellings terug na die gedeelde kant, net take verskil."** That restores
the form that stood before my morning change, and it dissolves the unsettled half too —
once interest is named among the shared criteria, the sentence no longer lends one
taxonomy's criterion to another taxonomy's groups, because interest is exactly what the
six are grouped on.

What survives from the morning check, and must not be re-used as a definition: interests
genuinely do differ from career to career *inside* one field. That is a true thing about
individual careers and a lesson may say it. It is not a true thing about what a field IS.

**The cost of the round trip: ten lessons.** Three carried the rejected career-field form,
and a drift sweep run straight after the ruling found five more shared terms adrift across
seven further lessons — one of them already delivered. **A shared term moved on one
lesson's evidence costs every lesson that carries it**, and the sweep is what makes that
cost visible, not the checks.

**Superseded — kept because the reasoning is what matters:** the wording stays as it is until then — a third wording is worse than a
known open question. The note is in the shared list and in Werk 3, 4 and 5 so a writer
meets it and does not silently repair it. Werk 3, 4 and 5 all carry the term.

lessons agree, and I prescribed the wording that became the next error. Also

evidence before I moved it on one lesson's check.
