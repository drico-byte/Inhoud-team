**Lampies, 8 October 2026.** Three video words kept four Grade 4 water lessons from passing
the fact check, because no source used them the way the video does:

- *invloei* for the water cycle's fourth step → **afloop (invloei)**
- *watersuiweringsentrum* → **watersuiweringsaanleg (watersuiweringsentrum)** (CAPS says *suiweringsaanlegte*)
- *riool* for the sewage itself → **rioolwater**, no brackets (*riool* is the pipe; CAPS says *rioolvuil*)

**Why:** the older rule — the text uses the video's word, because the video can't be remade —
assumed the video's word was a real term. When it isn't, copying it teaches a word nothing else
uses, and the fact check can never settle it. Brackets keep the link for a child who watches and
then reads, and the text stays true.

**How to apply:** when a video's word fails the fact check as a *term* (not as a claim), don't
park the lesson and don't keep re-checking — take the established word, CAPS's where CAPS has
one, and put the video's once in brackets. Change the shared-wordings list, the spec's ordering
fields and the protected-words file in the same breath; the old protection actively forbade the
fix. Recorded in the content standard's Gr 4 SW list. See [[gr4-geografie-besluite]],
[[wanneer-kaps-self-verkeerd-is]], [[bronne-weerspreek-mekaar-dek-ons-met-een-sin]].
