**Four things flagged into the planning briefs:**

  lesson's shell definition cost a revision round once already.

  language authority, and a people's own word for their own building is a different order
  of error. Also: CAPS writes "ledemaatbene is stutte", and **bene is forbidden for
  animals** in our house style.
