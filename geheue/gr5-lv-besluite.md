Lampies' Grade 5 Life Skills plan (workbook "Intersen LO", sheet G5-PSW, from CAPS p. 21-24): each CAPS topic is a teaching lesson plus a weekly reading piece. Rulings, 16 Sep 2026:

1. **Reading pieces: 500 words maximum, and they must be stories** — not the lesson retold in fewer words. CAPS asks learners to recall and retell, and a story with a clear order of events is what retells.
2. **Local health problems (CAPS 6 hours) gets an extra teaching lesson, split evenly**: two diseases each — TB and measles (person to person), diarrhoea and malaria (dirty water, mosquitoes). Reading piece unchanged.
3. **Religious festivals (4½ hours) gets an extra lesson and covers Grade 4's seven religions under the same names**: Judaisme, Christendom, Islam, Hindoeïsme, Boeddhisme, Baha'i-geloof, Afrika-religie. Two teaching lessons with one main festival each (check every example), plus one story reading piece set in one neighbourhood.
4. **Emotions and child abuse (4½ hours each) stay at two lessons**, the teaching lesson a bit longer.
5. **Be very careful about stereotypes in the age-and-gender-in-cultures pair** (sheet lessons 15-16): it comes six lessons after teaching what stereotyping is. Describe some families and communities, never a whole people.

6. **Length: 500 words for every lesson, teaching lessons and reading pieces alike; allowed range 400-500, now set in the content standard for Grade 5** ("since it's grade 5"; Grade 4 was 450). Not measured from the textbook, same as Grade 4. Lampies wants to hear AFTER planning which lessons might need an exception — propose them then, don't build them in beforehand.

So the year is **32 lessons**, not 30. The Grade 5 agreed-wordings file exists, empty on purpose, and says Grade 4 wordings are reused.

**Why:** decided in feedback on the workbook; Grade 4 Life Skills set the precedent of extra room for religions, and its religions lessons are the model.
**How to apply:** put 1, 3 and 5 into the planning brief as requirements. Also: sensitive topics (abuse, violence, substances) must never make the child responsible; health "treatment" means go to the clinic; phone numbers go stale ([[n-les-met-n-nommer-in-verouder]]); lesson on people who acted against child-rights violations uses only well-documented people ([[vertel-die-storie-moenie-dit-verklaar-nie]]).

## 18 Sep 2026, three more rulings

7. **A dense lesson does not buy extra words unless CAPS gave the block extra hours.** Lampies on the overloaded discrimination/stereotyping lesson: "Is the caps allocated hours more than the other lessons? If not, then we have to try keep it at 500." CAPS gives that block 3 hours, the ordinary allocation, so it stays at 500. This is the test for every future exception request.
8. **The HIV reading piece may have an adult character living with HIV**, telling it herself (a coach). Grade 4's no-character rule does not carry over: Grade 5's CAPS asks for changing attitudes, and an attitude changes towards someone.
9. **Illegal drugs ARE named** — reversing the planner's decision. "Focus on drugs that learners at that age gets exposed to most of the time": dagga, glue and other inhalants, tik, nyaope. Names and harm only; never how it feels, looks or is used. Candidate list, to be confirmed by the fact checker.

## 21 Sep 2026: finished, and the rulings made on the way

**All 32 lessons signed off with PDFs.** Rulings Lampies made along the way:
- Safety lessons (GO 1, 2, 8) raised to 550, following Drico's precedent in Grade 4.
- *afknouery* changed in both grades: "deur 'n ander kind of 'n groep kinders met meer mag".
- *middel* NARROWED in both grades (Lampies, 21 Sep, after three fact checks): "Iets anders as kos en water wat jy sluk, drink, inasem, aan jou vel smeer of ingespuit kry, en wat verander hoe jou liggaam of verstand werk, soos pille, alkohol of rook." This reverses the earlier "keep it".
- *voorouers* (both grades): "Die mense in jou familie van wie jy afstam en wat reeds oorlede is, soos 'n ouma of oupa wat al gesterf het."
- *tempel* (both grades): "'n Gebou waar Hindoes, Boeddhiste en mense van party ander gelowe aanbid."
- Grade 4 body safety: the clinic asks the child AND the adult; privacy at home is something a child MAY ASK for; the touching rules are framed around the asker ("touch or look at THEIR private parts"), "never as a game, never as a secret", no dentist, and the carer exception covers what health or safety needs.

**How to apply:** these are the agreed wordings in `kaps/`; a new lesson copies them word for word.
