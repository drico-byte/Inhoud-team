2026-09-03, Grade 4 Lewensvaardighede, the HIV lesson.

## What happened

The garden comparison had passed a full fact check at 48 of 48 claims and a
coverage check at 8 of 8 with all seven of its conditions satisfied. **Every
sentence in it was true.** Then the narrow ELI10 pre-check — the step that exists
to test the *mapping* rather than the claims — found this:

HIV goes into the fence posts. The posts go away one by one. The garden builds
new posts. And **nothing further is said about the virus.**

A nine-year-old who follows that through reaches the only answer the picture
leaves available: the old posts are gone, the new ones are clean, so the virus is
gone and the medicine is finished. That is false — HIV persists in a latent
reservoir that treatment does not clear, the reservoir does not shrink over
decades, and the virus rebounds as soon as treatment stops, which is why South
African policy is lifelong treatment.

**It is also the thought behind stopping treatment.** Of every wrong idea this
lesson could leave with a child who has to take medicine every day, that is the
worst one.

## Why nothing else could have caught it

- The **fact checker** verifies claims. There was no false claim — the defect was
  in the gap between the claims.
- The **coverage checker** verifies that what the spec asked for is present. The
  spec had not thought to ask for it, so nothing was missing.
- The **gate** measures. It measures nothing about meaning.

The defect lived in the inference a reader completes, and only an agent asked
*"where does a learner reasoning further end up?"* was looking there.

## What to do

**For any comparison, ask what the picture leaves unaccounted for.** Not "is each
sentence true" but "if a child follows this to its end, what is the only
conclusion available?" Every element introduced has to still be somewhere when the
picture closes. A thing that is put into the story and then quietly dropped gets
resolved by the reader, and the reader resolves it wrongly.

**Do not skip the ELI10 pre-check because the full fact check passed.** They are
not the same test, and on this lesson the full check passed the very block the
pre-check failed. Related: [[energie-as-stof-mag-as-beeld]] — the mapping still has
to be checked even when the image is permitted.

## And the fix has its own traps

Correcting this can reintroduce what an earlier round removed. Here, the obvious
repair — someone keeps the fence standing — brings back an outside agent replacing
posts, which reads as an endless race and teaches that the medicine only buys time.
The right shape was: the virus stays, and the medicine holds the posts only while
it is taken every day; the rebuilding is still the body's own. Both traps are now
numbered conditions in the spec, because a later revision would otherwise walk
straight back into them. See [[spesifikasies-word-nooit-nagegaan]].
