Gr 4 NWT Term 4, 2026-08-29. Three separate boundaries in one sub-topic's spec each
produced a false sentence, and all three failed the same way.

- **"Do not use the orbit to define a planet."** Without it, the definition described the
  Moon, which the lesson names two blocks earlier. With "any ball that orbits a star" it
  made Pluto a planet. There is no third wording.
- **"Do not say the Sun is a star."** Combined with the requirement about seeing stars in
  the evening, it forced the lesson to assert that no star is visible by day — false,
  since the Sun is one and is plainly visible.
- **"Do not say the Earth moves around the Sun."** It left the lesson saying the Earth
  circles "a star" and never which, so a child could finish believing it orbits some
  other star while the Sun hangs separately in the daytime sky.

**The shape.** Each ban was written to stop one lesson stealing another's content. Each
did it by forbidding a *fact* rather than by demarcating a *body of teaching*. A fact
cannot be withheld without the surrounding sentences bending around the hole, and what
bends is usually true-sounding and false.

**The rule that comes out of it: demarcate teaching, not facts.** "The Sun's size, heat,
distance and being our nearest star belong to Die Son" is a boundary that holds. "Do not
say the Sun is a star" is not — it forbids a true thing a neighbouring requirement needs.

**And they are expensive.** Each one cost a full revision round plus an escalation before
anyone saw that the spec was the cause and not the draft. The coverage checker cannot see
it, because the draft really does breach the spec; the fact checker cannot see it, because
it never reads the spec. Only the orchestrator holds both.

**A second-order failure worth its own line.** Amending a boundary does not remove it. The
29 August amendment to this spec left three superseded instructions behind in other
entries, each found later, each still able to reinstate the error. When a rule is
reversed, hunt the old rule everywhere — not only where the new one is written.
