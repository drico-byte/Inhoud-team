1doen elkeen 'n ander werk vir die operasiehet elkeen 'n ander taak by die operasienone
'Elkeen doen 'n ander werk vir die operasie' is natural Afrikaans, and the swap from 'vir' to 'by' moves each discovery from serving the operation to being present at it, so this is a lateral move with a small meaning shift.
2kan kieme by die wond komkan kieme in die wond komnone
'By die wond kom' is the idiomatic 'reach/get at the wound', which is exactly the point while the chest is open; 'in die wond kom' changes reaching to entering, so the preposition swap moves the meaning without fixing a fault.
