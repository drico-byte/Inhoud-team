'versprei' is the natural Afrikaans verb for copies passing from place to place, and the meaning is unchanged.
5Ander lesers het Marco Polo geglo en het sy verslagAnder lesers het Marco Polo wel geglo en sy verslagThe contrast with the previous sentence is unmarked and the repeated 'het' in the joined clause is clumsy.
'wel' marks the contrast a fluent writer would mark, and dropping the second 'het' is cleaner, with the same people and actions.
