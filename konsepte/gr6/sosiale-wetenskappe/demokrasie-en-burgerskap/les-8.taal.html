'Sommer alles wat jy kan kry' is already natural Afrikaans and an editor would let it pass; the idiom is a lateral move, and dropping 'kan' shifts the sense slightly from everything you can find to whatever happens to come your way.

Skryf jou biografie in tydsorde van haar vroeë jare af. Leef sy nog, loop dit tot vandag toe.
'haar' and 'sy' come before any antecedent: the person written about is only introduced in the last sentence of the block ('Die mens oor wie jy skryf'), and nothing says the person is a woman. The lesson keeps using sy/haar right through (lys, 'n onderhoud met haar'). Whether the person is meant to be a woman, or the pronoun should become neutral, is a content decision, so I have not changed it; moving the last sentence of the block up would also fix the reference.
Protected word primêre bron - "... Dit bly 'n primêre bron, ook as sy dit jare later vertel.": Read alone as a glossary entry, 'sy' has no clear antecedent: the sentence before names 'die mens self, of iemand wat dit gesien het', and 'sy' could be read as either of them or as the source. A neutral 'die mens' or 'hulle' would make the entry stand alone; this is a decided shared wording, so it is for a person to judge.
