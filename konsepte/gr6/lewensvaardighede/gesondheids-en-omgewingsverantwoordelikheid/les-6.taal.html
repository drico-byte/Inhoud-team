1iemand wat siek is hoes of niesiemand wat siek is, hoes of niesTwo verbs of different clauses collide ('is hoes'), so a Grade 6 reader stumbles on first reading.
The AWS comma between colliding verbs makes the sentence easy to read and changes nothing in its meaning.
3nog voor die kolle gewys hetnog voordat die kolle verskyn het'gewys het' is an unidiomatic verb for spots appearing.
'verskyn het' is the right verb for the action and 'voordat' introduces the clause cleanly; the timing is unchanged.
4so dit is nie jou skuld niedaarom is dit nie jou skuld nie'so' as an uninverted 'therefore' conjunction copies English.
'daarom is dit' is the natural Afrikaans construction and keeps the same reasoning in the nurse's reassurance.
5'n merk hou'n merk oorhou'n merk hou' is a loose form of the fixed expression for a mark that remains.
'n merk oorhou' is the idiomatic expression and says the same thing.
6Ek gee net wat die kliniek sê, en niks anders nie.Ek gee net wat die kliniek sê en niks anders nie.A comma stands before 'en' where the second part has no subject and verb of its own.
AWS 2017 takes no comma here; punctuation only, the wording of what Ma gives is untouched.
7Haar klein broer ThaboHaar boetie Thabo'klein broer' calques English 'little brother'.
'boetie' is the natural Afrikaans word for a younger brother and the same person is meant.
9dat haar keel seer geword hetdat haar keel toegetrek het'keel seer geword' reads as an illness symptom in a story about a sick child, blurring the emotional sense.
'keel het toegetrek' is the fixed Afrikaans expression for the emotional tightness the sentence means.
10Na die oproepNá die oproep'Na' meaning 'after' lacks the acute that distinguishes it from 'na' meaning 'to', which occurs nearby ('na haar arms gekyk').
AWS spelling 'Ná' is a plain spelling fix with no change in meaning.
