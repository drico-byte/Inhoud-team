13As jy nog nie verskil sien nieAs jy nog nie 'n verskil sien nie'nog nie verskil sien nie' lacks the article Afrikaans needs here.
Adding 'n is a plain grammar fix with no change in meaning.

Iets wat jou aandag trek: jy doen dit graag of jy wil meer daarvan weet, of jy dit nou al kan doen of nie.
By AWS 2017 a comma belongs before the first 'of' (two clauses with their own subject and verb), as proposed for the study text; the glossary text also occurs inside the study sentence, so no unique span exists, and it is probably a shared definition, so a person or writer should decide whether the definition gets the same comma.
Wanneer jy iets wil leer, is 'n bron iets of iemand waarby jy dit kan leer.
'waarby' fits 'iets' but strictly 'by wie' is the form for 'iemand'; acceptable in everyday use, but noted in case the shared definition is revisited.
