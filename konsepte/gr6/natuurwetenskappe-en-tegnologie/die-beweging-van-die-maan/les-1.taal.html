#VoorNáFault and reason
1'n lyn deur sy middel waarom hy draai.'n lyn deur sy middel. Hy draai om daardie lyn.Relative 'waarom' (around which) is easily read as 'why' by a Grade 6 reader, so the sentence makes them stumble.
Splitting the sentence keeps the same meaning (the axis is a line through its middle that it turns around) and removes the stumble.
4Teen die tyd dat jy terug isWanneer jy weer by jou beginplek is'Teen die tyd dat' copies English 'by the time', and 'terug is' leaves where the learner returns to unsaid.
'Wanneer jy weer by jou beginplek is' is natural Afrikaans, clearer, and keeps the meaning and the following clause intact.
5sou ons in een reis om die Aarde al sy kante siensou ons al sy kante sien terwyl hy een keer om die Aarde reis'Sou ons in een reis om die Aarde' leaves unclear who travels and can read as if we do.
The proposal makes the Moon the traveller with an unambiguous 'hy', without changing what is claimed.
6Ons sien altyd dieselfde kant en daaromOns sien altyd dieselfde kant, en daaromMissing AWS 2017 comma before 'en' joining two clauses with their own subject and verb ('Ons sien' / 'daarom weet ons').
Required comma; matches the comma already used in 'Die Maan wentel om die Aarde, en die twee reis'.
7die Maan se fases elke 29 en 'n half dae herhaaldie Maan se fases hulle elke 29 en 'n half dae herhaal'Herhaal' is transitive in standard Afrikaans and needs the reflexive pronoun here.
Adding the reflexive 'hulle' supplies a word the grammar requires without touching the number or the meaning.
