#VoorNáFault and reason
1Die Son is 'n ster en elke sterDie Son is 'n ster, en elke sterMissing AWS comma before 'en' joining two full clauses ('Die Son is 'n ster' / 'elke ster maak sy eie lig').
Both clauses have their own subject and verb, so the AWS 2017 comma is required and nothing else changes.
2val en weer van hulle af terugbonsval en van hulle af terugbons'weer ... terugbons' is pleonastic: 'terug' already says the light comes back.
Dropping the redundant 'weer' carries no information away, leaves the protected 'Ons sien hulle omdat' intact and brings the sentence in line with the definition of weerkaats.

The proposal is natural Afrikaans, says the same thing, repeats the lesson's own phrasing 'nie sy eie lig maak nie', and 'hom' in the next sentence still points to the wall.
6berge en hoë landberge en hooglande'hoë land' as an uncountable phrase is loose and wooden beside 'berge'.
'hooglande' is the natural Afrikaans word for the same high-lying land, a Grade 6 reader knows it, and it touches no protected word ('vlaktes' stays).
