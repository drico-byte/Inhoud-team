1onsself en ons klere en skottelgoedonsself, ons klere en skottelgoedThe repeated 'en ... en' makes a plain list of three read wooden and briefly ambiguous.
The comma list is the natural Afrikaans form for three items and keeps the same three things.
2om produkte te maak en hul afvalwaterom produkte te maak, en hul afvalwaterMissing AWS 2017 comma: 'en' joins two clauses with their own subject and verb ('Fabrieke gebruik' / 'hul afvalwater kan bevat').
The comma is required and stops the reader running 'om produkte te maak en hul afvalwater' together.
3van huise en fabrieke en plasevan huise, fabrieke en plaseThe repeated 'en ... en' in a list of three is clumsy.
Same three sources in a normal comma list; the meaning is unchanged.
4voordat dit terug na 'n rivier of 'n ander waterbron gaanvoordat dit na 'n rivier of 'n ander waterbron teruggaan'terug na ... gaan' splits the verb in an English-shaped way at the end of a subordinate clause.
'na ... teruggaan' is the tighter Afrikaans order, matches the glossary definition, and says the same thing.
6om water nie in die eerste plek te besoedel nieom te verhoed dat water besoedel word'in die eerste plek' is a translation of 'in the first place'; in Afrikaans it means 'firstly'.
'om te verhoed dat water besoedel word' says the same point naturally and keeps the protected word 'besoedel'.
