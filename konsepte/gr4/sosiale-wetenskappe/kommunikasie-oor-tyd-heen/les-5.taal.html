#VoorNáFault and reason
1'n Kantoor se briewe isIn 'n kantoor is brieweThe possessive opener "'n Kantoor se briewe is met die hand geskryf" is stiff.
The locative "In 'n kantoor is briewe" is the natural Afrikaans opening, costs one word and says the same thing.
2Soms is die woord net doodgetrekSoms is die verkeerde woord net doodgetrek"die woord" has no antecedent; the sentence before speaks of 'n fout, not a word.
"die verkeerde woord" pairs cleanly with "die regte een" and removes a stumble for one word, with no change in meaning.
3en dit is waar die naam QWERTY vandaan komen daarvandaan kom die naam QWERTY"dit is waar ... vandaan kom" copies English and can first be misread as "dit is waar" (that is true).
"daarvandaan kom die naam QWERTY" is idiomatic, shorter and keeps QWERTY intact; the comma stays correct because both clauses have their own subject and verb.
4elke letter het presies dieselfde vorm gehadelke letter het elke keer presies dieselfde vorm gehad"elke letter het presies dieselfde vorm gehad" is imprecise: same as what, as if all letters shared one shape.
"elke keer" states what the reason actually means (a letter always printed the same) without adding a fact, for two words.

"Ons gebruik die QWERTY-uitleg vandag nog" is plain natural Afrikaans, one word shorter, with the same meaning and QWERTY unchanged.

7sonder dat iemand een woord oorgeskryf hetsonder dat iemand een woord hoef oor te skryfnone
