1help jou later onthouhelp jou om later te onthouThe bare infinitive with an adverb wedged in ('help jou later onthou') reads loosely in written text.
The full 'om ... te' form is clearer for a Grade 4 reader and the meaning is unchanged.
2groot en swaar en moeilikgroot, swaar en moeilikThe repeated 'en' in a three-part list reads wooden.
A comma list is the natural written form, matches the heading, and keeps all three qualities.

'Opgestel' is the exact verb for setting a camera up on a tripod; the protected word 'driepoot' is untouched.

The proposal fixes tense and style; 'ook' carries the joining sense of 'En' without changing the meaning.
