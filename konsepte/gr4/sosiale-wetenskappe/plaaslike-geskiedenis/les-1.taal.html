Protected word voorwerpbronne: Asked explicitly: it is a regular, transparent Afrikaans compound (voorwerp + bron, like 'waterbron' or 'inligtingsbron'), and a nine-year-old can take it apart without help, so it is acceptable Afrikaans even if no source uses it. Protected word Soms wys hulle nie genoeg nie en die vraag bly oop.: By the AWS 2017 rule in this prompt, a comma belongs before 'en' here: 'hulle wys' and 'die vraag bly' are two clauses, each with its own subject and verb ('Soms wys hulle nie genoeg nie, en die vraag bly oop.'). Not changed because the wording is protected.
