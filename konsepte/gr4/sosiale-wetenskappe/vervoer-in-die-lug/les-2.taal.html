1Op hul sweeftuie het hulle geleerMet hul sweeftuie het hulle geleernone
The preposition swap moves the meaning: 'op' says they learned while flying aboard the gliders, which is natural Afrikaans ('op 'n fiets leer ry'), while 'met' says they learned by means of them, so the change is at best lateral.
2Met die enjin en sterk wind konMet die enjin en 'n sterk wind konnone
'Sterk wind' as an uncountable noun is idiomatic Afrikaans ('daar was sterk wind'), so adding the article is a lateral move that only adds a word.
