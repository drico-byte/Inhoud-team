1dit oop en toe getrekdit oop- en toegetrekSeparable compound verbs sharing a participle are written apart and without the hyphen ('oop en toe getrek').
'oop- en toegetrek' is the correct spelling and the meaning is unchanged.
2verbygehardloop en hy hetverbygehardloop, en Karabo hetThe second 'hy' could refer to Sipho or Karabo, since both were just named.
Naming Karabo, already in the paragraph, removes the ambiguity, keeps the next 'Hy' pointing at Karabo, and the comma is correct because both clauses have their own subject and verb.
3Van die ander kinders het gedinkParty van die ander kinders het gedinkPartitive 'Van die' opening a sentence can read as a broken prepositional phrase to a Grade 4 reader.
'Party van die' is plainer standard Afrikaans and keeps the same 'some of' qualifier.
4Die skoot het geklap en die kindersDie skoot het geklap, en die kindersMissing AWS 2017 comma before 'en' joining two clauses with their own subject and verb.
'Die skoot het geklap' and 'die kinders het weggeskiet' are full clauses, so the comma is required.
