#VoorNáFault and reason
1bo-oor party winkels se deurebo die deure van party winkels'Bo-oor' means over or across the top of something, which is imprecise for letters that sit above a door.
'Bo die deure van party winkels' names the same place more exactly in plain words; nothing is added or lost.
3Die dik een staan swaar en die dun een lyk lig.Die dik een lyk swaar, en die dun een lyk lig.Two clauses with their own subject and verb are joined by 'en' without the AWS comma, and 'staan swaar' is a stiff pairing next to 'lyk lig'.
The comma is required by the AWS 2017, and 'lyk swaar' parallels 'lyk lig' with the same meaning of looking heavy.
5Die oop plek rondom die letterDie oop ruimte rondom die letterThe block names the concept 'oop ruimte' and the sentence before uses it, so switching to 'oop plek' can suggest a second idea to a Grade 4 reader.
Using the block's own term for the same thing keeps the concept consistent without changing what the sentence says.
6In 'n patroon herhaal dieselfde vorm oor die hele mat.In 'n patroon word dieselfde vorm oor die hele mat herhaal.Intransitive 'herhaal' ('dieselfde vorm herhaal') copies English 'repeats'; standard Afrikaans uses 'herhaal hom' or the passive.
The passive is the standard form and matches the protected glossary wording 'herhaal word'; the meaning is unchanged (note that the lesson title and one heading still use 'wat herhaal', which this change does not touch).
8'n lig agtergrond'n ligte agtergrondThe attributive adjective is uninflected: 'n lig agtergrond.
'n Ligte agtergrond' is the required inflected form; a plain grammar fix.
9oor die hele prent herhaal.oor die hele prent herhaal word.Intransitive 'herhaal' ('watter vorm ... herhaal') copies English 'repeats'.
The passive 'herhaal word' is standard Afrikaans, matches change 6 and the protected glossary wording, and keeps the meaning.

'Aanbeweeg' is a valid word whose sense of moving on from one shape to the next fits the sentence; plain 'beweeg' loses that progression and is not clearly better.
