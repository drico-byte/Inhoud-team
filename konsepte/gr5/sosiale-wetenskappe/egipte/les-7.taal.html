4Sy graf het nog niemand gevind nie.Maar sy graf was nog nie gevind nie.With the object first, 'Sy graf het nog niemand gevind nie' can first be read as the grave not having found anyone.
The passive removes the misreading, says the same thing (the grave had not yet been found), and 'Maar' only makes explicit the contrast already there with the objects found.

Naming the grave fixes the ambiguous pronoun without changing the meaning, and the later 'dit' in the same sentence then clearly refers to the grave too.
