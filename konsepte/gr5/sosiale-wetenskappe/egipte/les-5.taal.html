#VoorNáFault and reason
1Twee van hulle draTwee daarvan dra'hulle' is used twice in one sentence for two different things (the scrolls, then the men's object), so a reader can stumble over who bought whom.
'Twee daarvan' is the idiomatic form for things and leaves the second 'hulle' as the only pronoun, with the meaning unchanged.
2meer as 150 jaar terugmeer as 150 jaar gelede'jaar terug' is colloquial, and 'terug gekoop' can be misread as 'teruggekoop' (bought back).
'jaar gelede' is the standard written form and removes the misreading without changing the meaning.
4Die kanale dra bloed, lug en ander soorte vloeistofDie kanale dra bloed, ander soorte vloeistof en lug'lug en ander soorte vloeistof' implies that air is a kind of liquid.
Reordering keeps exactly the same three items and removes the false implication, without touching the protected 'het geglo' sentence.
5Vir hulle het dit sin gemaakVir hulle was dit sinvol'sin maak' is a calque of English 'make sense'.
'Vir hulle was dit sinvol' is the standard Afrikaans form with the same meaning, and the 'want' clause still follows naturally.
