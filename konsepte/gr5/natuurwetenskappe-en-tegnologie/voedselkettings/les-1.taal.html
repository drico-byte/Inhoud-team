#VoorNáFault and reason
1groter en kry hygroter, en kry hyMissing AWS 2017 comma before 'en' joining two clauses with their own subject and verb (boom/word, hy/kry).
The second clause has its own subject 'hy' and verb 'kry', so the comma is required; the fronted 'Tog' does not change that.
2Die tweede is 'n gas in die lug met die naam koolstofdioksied.Die tweede ding is 'n gas in die lug wat koolstofdioksied genoem word.'met die naam' is a wooden, English-like construction, and 'Die tweede' breaks the parallel with 'Die eerste ding' and 'Die derde ding'.
'wat koolstofdioksied genoem word' is natural Afrikaans, the protected word stays intact and the meaning is unchanged.
4kos uit die lug en waterkos uit lug en waterThe article on only one of the two nouns ('die lug en water') is unbalanced and differs from the glossary's 'uit lug en water'.
Dropping the article balances the pair and matches the lesson's own fotosintese wording without changing the meaning or touching a protected word.
5Sonder kos kan 'n dier nie beweeg nie en ook nie groei nie.Sonder kos kan 'n dier nie beweeg of groei nie.The doubled 'nie ... nie en ook nie ... nie' is clumsy for a Grade 5 reader.
One negation over 'beweeg of groei' says exactly the same thing, is shorter and reads more naturally.
6'n Leeu is 'n karnivoor en hy jag ander diere vir sy kos.'n Leeu is 'n karnivoor wat ander diere vir sy kos jag.Two clauses with their own subjects joined by 'en' without the AWS comma, and 'en hy' reads flat next to the parallel cow and pig sentences.
The relative clause keeps the same lion, the same action and the same meaning, removes the comma problem and matches the pattern of the neighbouring blocks; the following 'Sy' still points to the lion.
