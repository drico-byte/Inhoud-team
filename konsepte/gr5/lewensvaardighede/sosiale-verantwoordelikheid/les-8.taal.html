#VoorNáFault and reason
1dit is nou reg en dek die bakdit is nou reg, en sy dek die bakThe second verb 'dek' has no subject of its own, so on a first reading it can attach to 'dit' (the dough) instead of to Ouma.
Giving 'dek' its own subject 'sy' makes clear that Ouma covers the bowl, without changing what happens, and the comma before 'en' is the AWS comma between two full clauses.
2sodra sy die eerste paar keer druksodra sy die deeg die eerste paar keer druk'Druk' stands without an object, so the reader has to work out what Ouma is pressing.
Naming the dough already in her bowl fixes the vague verb and adds no new fact, since that is what she is pressing.
4dus stap Palesadaarom stap Palesa'Dus' reads like a formal conclusion in an argument, which is stiff in a Grade 5 story.
'Daarom' is the natural spoken word for the same cause and effect, with the same word order and the same meaning.
