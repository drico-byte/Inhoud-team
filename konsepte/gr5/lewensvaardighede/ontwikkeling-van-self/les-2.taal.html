#VoorNáWhy
2het nog in die rubber vasgesteekhet nog in die rubber gesteeknone
'Vasgesteek' is ordinary Afrikaans for something lodged firmly, which is why she has to pull it out with her nails; plain 'gesteek' loses that and can read as 'stabbed' to a Grade 5 reader.
3met die grondpad na die dam gerymet die grondpad langs na die dam gerynone
'Met die grondpad gery' is normal Afrikaans for taking a road (as in 'met die N1 ry'), so adding 'langs' only makes it longer without improving it.
