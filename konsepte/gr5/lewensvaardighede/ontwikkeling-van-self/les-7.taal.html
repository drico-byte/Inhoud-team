Literally a gift 'is told' later; it is the surprise or secret about the gift that is told. 'later vertel' is protected, so this is flagged rather than changed.
Elkeen van hierdie tekens is op sy eie genoeg, ook al voel die verhouding lekker.
'Genoeg' is vague: it does not say enough for what (presumably to make the relationship unsafe); fixing it would mean guessing the intended wording.
Protected word Dieselfde tekens van onveilig geld ook vir mense wat jy goed ken: 'tekens van onveilig' uses the adjective as a noun, which is unidiomatic; 'tekens van onveiligheid' or 'tekens dat iets onveilig is' would be natural. The same phrase recurs unprotected in 'Ouer mense in jou lewe' and was left alone there for consistency.
