# Writer brief: faults the HTML team found in delivered V2 lessons (18 November 2026)

The HTML team builds every V2 lesson exactly as approved and reports what looks wrong. The items
below were judged real faults. For EACH lesson in your launch message:

1. Read the draft (the path below) and its spec extract `spek/les-N.json` beside it.
2. Fix exactly the fault described. Describe-and-choose: choose the wording yourself. Change
   nothing else. Keep agreed glossary wordings (the injected list) word for word and keep every
   protected wording (they are listed in the extract's protected-word material if any).
3. Re-read the changed block whole: a fix disturbs its neighbours.
4. Stay under the budget x 1.12. Record the change, dated 18 November 2026, in the provenance
   note ("HTML-span navraag Nxx"). Status stays as the writer convention requires (`konsep`).

No textbook. Do not edit any spec. Report one line per lesson.

## The faults

- **N02 - `konsepte/gr4/sosiale-wetenskappe/kaartvaardighede/les-8.json`** (block "Begin by Afrika"):
  "Begin by Afrika want ons eie land lê op daardie kontinent." joins two clauses each with its own
  subject and verb with "want", so AWS 2017 requires a comma before "want".
- **N04 - `konsepte/gr4/sosiale-wetenskappe/kommunikasie-oor-tyd-heen/les-11.json`** (list "Die nuwe
  maniere, een ná die ander"): the list ends "Toe die internet." then "En toe die selfoon.", but the
  lesson itself says portable phones could be bought in the early 1980s, before the internet reached
  homes. The order contradicts the lesson's own first block. Make the list's order true and
  consistent with the lesson (state what the order is of, if that helps), without adding dates.
- **N06 - `konsepte/gr4/natuurwetenskappe-en-tegnologie/energie-en-energie-oordrag/les-1.json`**:
  "Daar bly dit gebere ..." - the word is "gebêre" (lesson 19 writes it correctly).
- **N07 - `konsepte/gr4/natuurwetenskappe-en-tegnologie/lewende-en-nie-lewende-dinge/les-1.json`**: the
  glossary defines "waterdamp" but the study text never uses the word. Either drop the entry, or use
  the word where the text already talks about it - whichever keeps the lesson true and inside its
  spec (check the spec's expected terms before dropping it).
- **N15 - `konsepte/gr6/sosiale-wetenskappe/demokrasie-en-burgerskap/les-8.json`** (block "Wat 'n
  biografie is"): "Skryf jou biografie in tydsorde van haar vroeë jare af. Leef sy nog, ..." -
  "haar"/"sy" refer to a person the paragraph introduces only in its last sentence. Reorder or
  rephrase so the person is introduced before the pronouns.
- **N17 - `konsepte/gr6/sosiale-wetenskappe/medisyne-oor-tyd-heen/les-5.json`** (block "Pasteur se
  twee soorte flesse"): the text does not say how the second kind of flask differed. Name it: the
  second flasks had a long neck bent like a swan's neck (S-shape), which let air in but trapped the
  dust before it reached the liquid. The picture shows this. Keep it short and true.
- **N19, N20 - `konsepte/gr6/sosiale-wetenskappe/klimaat-en-plantegroei-oor-die-wereld/les-6.json`**:
  (a) "Op die temperatuurkaarte lê hierdie bande in die warm dele." holds only in each hemisphere's
  summer (the Sahara averages about 14 °C in January). Tie it to the summer / the right month's map.
  (b) "Op baie reënvalkaarte het hulle van die ligste skakerings, net soos die poolstreke." is true of
  Antarctica but not of the whole Arctic. Narrow it (e.g. Antarktika).
- **N21 - `konsepte/gr6/sosiale-wetenskappe/klimaat-en-plantegroei-oor-die-wereld/les-3.json`**: "Die
  Sahara is op die temperatuurkaarte ook warm, maar op die reënvalkaart baie droog." - on the January
  map most of the Sahara is not in the warm colours; it is warm on the July map / in its summer.
- **N22 - `konsepte/gr6/sosiale-wetenskappe/klimaat-en-plantegroei-oor-die-wereld/les-8.json`**: "Twee
  kaarte is dus nodig om hierdie plek se hele jaar te wys." follows a rainfall sentence, so a learner
  may read the two maps as rainfall + temperature; it means the January and the July temperature
  maps. Make that clear.
