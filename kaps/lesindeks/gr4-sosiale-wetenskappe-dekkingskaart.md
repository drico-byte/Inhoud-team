# Gr 4 Sosiale Wetenskappe — coverage map, Geskiedenis

Every CAPS item for Grade 4 Geskiedenis against the lesson the video script claims
it. Built 7 September 2026, from the CAPS document and the four lesson-labelled
scripts. **Produced before anything is written**, which is the standing rule: the
CAPS document contradicts itself between its parts often enough that a plan matching
one part can still miss what another assesses.

Read the columns as: what CAPS names · which script lesson covers it · what to check.

## Kwartaal 1 — Plaaslike geskiedenis (15 h) · script has 5 lessons, 1 453 words

CAPS focus: *"Vind meer uit oor die verlede en die toepassing van hierdie kennis op
die plaaslike geskiedenis."*

| CAPS item | Hours | Script lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Hoe kan ons oor die **hede** uitvind in 'n plaaslike gebied — foto's, geskrifte, stories en onderhoude met mense, voorwerpe | 2 | Les 2 (four source types), Les 3 (worked examples) |
| Hoe kan ons oor die **geskiedenis** uitvind van 'n plaaslike gebied — the same four | 3 | Les 2, Les 3 |
| **Geskiedenisprojek vir Graad 4: Plaaslike Geskiedenis** | **7** | **nothing** |
| Hersiening en assessering | 3 | n/a — not our content |

### BESLIS: die plaaslike-gebied-anker word laat vaar

**Drico, 7 September 2026: "Lets just ignore the local area (just trust me for now)."**
Recorded here so it is a decision and not an omission. A coverage checker reads the
CAPS bullets, and every one of them says *in 'n plaaslike gebied* — without this note
it would report the same gap on every lesson, every round, and the obvious fix would
be to put back something Drico has ruled out.

The concern was raised and he ruled. It is reviewable, and the argument for reopening
it, if it ever needs reopening, is that CAPS says *plaaslike gebied* five times and
its focus sentence is about applying the knowledge to local history. The argument the
other way is that a *local* area cannot be taught by a national video or a national
text at all.

The seven-hour project below is a separate question and is **not** covered by this
ruling.

**Two findings, and the second is the largest hole in the whole subject.**

The script's four source types line up well with CAPS's four bullets — visuele
matches foto's, skriftelike matches geskrifte, mondelinge matches stories en
onderhoude, argeologiese matches voorwerpe. That mapping is sound.

**But the script is generic where CAPS is local.** Every CAPS bullet here says *in 'n
plaaslike gebied*, and the focus sentence says the point is applying the knowledge to
local history. The script teaches what a historian does and what sources are, with no
local anchor at all. A learner could finish it without ever looking at their own town.

**And CAPS gives 7 of 15 hours to a local-history project**, which nothing covers.
That is nearly half the term. A project is a teacher's activity rather than reading
material, so it may be right that we carry none of it — but that is a decision to
make out loud, not by omission. Note also that Kwartaal 1's formal assessment for
Geskiedenis is a **Projek**, the only term where it is.

Script lessons with no CAPS bullet behind them: **Les 1** (what history is, detective
comparison), **Les 4** (history concepts), **Les 5** (why learn history). None is
wrong — they serve the CAPS *skills* in Section 2.4.3 rather than a content bullet —
but they are supplement and should carry a justification.

## Kwartaal 2 — Leer van leiers (15 h) · script has 3 lessons, 1 746 words

CAPS focus: *"Die eienskappe van goeie leiers, deur van die lewensverhale van Nelson
Mandela en Mahatma Gandhi as voorbeelde gebruik te maak."*

| CAPS item | Hours | Script lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Stel en beantwoord vrae oor die lewe en die eienskappe van goeie leiers — **eight named qualities**: luister na die mense; is 'n dienaar van die mense; werk met 'n span; het moed; is dapper; is toegewyd aan sy oortuigings; is toegewyd aan ander; is bereid om iets ter wille van ander op te offer | 2 | Les 1 |
| Lewensverhale van leiers wat daardie eienskappe toon | — | Les 2 (Gandhi), Les 3 (Mandela) |
| The four framing questions: Is dit altyd maklik om 'n goeie leier te wees? Is leiers altyd gewild? Is leiers altyd volmaak? Hoe kan gewone mense die voorbeeld van goeie leiers volg? | — | **check** |
| Hersiening en assessering | 3 | n/a |

This topic aligns best of the four. Three things to check when it is planned:

**The eight qualities are content, not decoration.** CAPS lists them as bullets, which
is the most prescriptive it gets, so all eight are mandatory. Les 1 must carry them
all or they must be spread across the three lessons deliberately.

**The four framing questions are also CAPS bullets** — including *"Is leiers altyd
volmaak?"*, which is the one a lesson is most likely to skip. They are not questions
for the learner to answer on the page (our lessons carry no questions); they are
content the text has to address.

**CAPS permits an added leader** — local, provincial, national or international — if
sources and time allow. Optional, so it needs a justification if taken.

**Twelve CAPS bullets, eleven script lessons.** One deliberate merge (Rekenaar +
Internet in Les 10), which is the same shape as the transport merges and is fine.

**One title needs checking before planning.** CAPS names *Selfoon* as its own bullet.
Les 11 is called *"Die telefoon en sosiale media"* while Les 7 is already *"Die
telefoon"*. Either Les 11 is the cellphone lesson and its title is loose, or the
cellphone is not covered — and CAPS names it, so it is mandatory.

**Two script lessons have no CAPS bullet: Les 2 (antieke skryfstelsels — hierogliewe,
wigskrif, Griekse alfabet) and the social-media half of Les 11.** Both are supplement
needing a justification. The ancient writing one has a strong case: CAPS's *"Taal,
simbole"* bullet in the oldest-forms section reaches toward it. Social media has no
CAPS anchor at all.

**And check Les 1 covers all five things** CAPS names in that bullet — taal, simbole,
liedere, kuns **and dans**. Song and dance are the two most likely to fall out of a
lesson about the San.

## The curriculum document does not match the scripts

`Geskiedenis Kurrikulum.docx` describes **eight themes** under *"Toeka tot Nou"* —
Hoe leer jy oor geskiedenis, Vervoer, Kommunikasie, Handel oor tyd, Medisyne oor tyd,
Moderne uitvindings, Afrikaans oor tyd, and a summative Lewe in SA — two per term,
with only three marked as prescribed Grade 4 CAPS themes.

**The scripts do not follow it.** They are exactly the four CAPS Grade 4 topics,
including *Leer van leiers*, which is absent from the eight themes, and none of the
five non-CAPS themes has a script.

So the scripts follow CAPS and the curriculum document describes something else —
possibly an earlier plan, possibly a wider ambition. **Which one governs is a decision
for Drico**, and it decides whether this subject is 29 lessons or something much
larger.
